Why Every Page Includes the Manual Math

Most converter sites just hand you a number. converters.zzml.com also shows you the formula, a step-by-step worked example, common mistakes, and a reference chart on every page. That means you learn the conversion instead of memorizing a link — and the richer content also makes each page genuinely useful as a reference you come back to.

The conversion factors used across the site are the international exact values where they exist: 1 inch = 2.54 cm, 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg, 1 US gallon = 3.785411784 liters, and so on. Where systems genuinely differ — like US versus UK gallons, or US versus Australian tablespoons — each page explains the difference so you never get caught out.

Calculators for Real Decisions

The calculators on calc.zzml.com are built for the questions that actually come up: "What will this loan really cost me per month?", "How much should I tip and how do we split it?", "Is my BMI in a healthy range?", "How long until my savings reach a target?" Each one uses standard, widely accepted formulas, and every result page explains what the numbers mean so you can act on them.

Notes on accuracy

  • Loan and savings calculators use standard amortization and compound interest formulas, rounding only for display.
  • BMI uses the standard WHO classification categories.
  • Results are estimates for general use, not financial or medical advice — always confirm important decisions with a professional.
  • All calculations happen locally, so you can enter real numbers without privacy concerns.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is everything on zzml.com really free?

Yes. Every tool, converter, calculator and guide across the network is completely free. There are no premium versions, no paywalls, and no feature restrictions. The sites are supported by minimal advertising.

Does zzml.com collect my data?

No. All tools and calculators run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Data you enter never leaves your device, so there is nothing for anyone to collect.

What sites are part of the zzml.com network?

The network includes tool.zzml.com (developer tools), converters.zzml.com (unit converters), calc.zzml.com (everyday calculators), facts.zzml.com (reference library), and homehacks.zzml.com (household guides). All are reachable from this homepage.

Do I need an account to use these tools?

No account is needed anywhere on the network. Just open a page and start using the tool immediately.

Can I bookmark or share specific tool pages?

Absolutely. Every tool and reference page has its own permanent URL, so you can bookmark it or share a direct link with friends, family, or colleagues.
